# QA checklist (before delivery)

If any item cannot be satisfied, note the reason in the report and flag it as a gap:

- All seven signal categories are represented (use “No data available” when empty)
- Overall health status matches the most severe individual finding
- TL;DR present and readable aloud in ≤30 seconds
- Every action item names a specific component and a concrete next step
- ATTENTION REQUIRED banner when CRITICAL or multiple DEGRADED signals
- Data scoped only to the defined area of responsibility
- Trend comparisons included for key metrics
- No-data states reported explicitly
